About QuizNest

A little bit about the project

QuizNest is a timed online quiz platform built as a second-year front-end development project. It brings together a bubble-sheet scantron look with a simple, fast quiz-taking experience across categories like Art & Literature, Geography, History, Science & Nature, Sports, and more.

The goal of QuizNest is to let anyone create a quiz in minutes and share it with friends, or jump straight into a category and test what they know. This project was built using HTML, CSS, and a touch of JavaScript-free, CSS-only interactivity for things like the navigation menu and search bar.
